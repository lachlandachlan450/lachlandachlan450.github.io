Testing Monitoring Models with Encoded Transcripts

2026 Apart AI Control Hackathon · writeup

Stress-tested chain-of-thought monitorability by feeding Claude Haiku 4.5 transcripts of malicious activity under different encodings, and found monitoring degraded on half-encoded transcripts more than fully encoded ones.

LLM Continuous Probability Inference

Low probability estimation · code

We try to use an energy-based sampling method to estimate the probability of rare outputs of an LLM while it is in the training phase, as distribution shift of the inputs in deployment can dramatically alter the probability of outputs. Therefore we need to be able to tractably estimate very rare outputs in the training phase.
